## YouTube Monetization: Overview

This document provides a clear guide to enabling YouTube monetization, including the prerequisites, application steps, and some tips for success.

---

### **Prerequisites for YouTube Monetization**
1. **YouTube Partner Program Requirements:**
   - Your channel must follow all of YouTube's monetization policies.
   - Reside in a country where the YouTube Partner Program is available.

2. **Eligibility Thresholds:**
   - At least 1,000 subscribers.
   - Public watch hours:
     - **Standard monetization:** 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months.
     - **Shorts bonus eligibility (if applicable):** 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days.

3. **Linked Google AdSense Account:**
   - Create or connect a valid AdSense account for payment.

4. **Account Status (Good Standing):**
   - No active community guideline strikes.

---

### Steps to Enable Monetization

1. **Prepare Your Content:**
   - Publish quality, engaging videos consistently.
   - Optimize descriptions, titles, and thumbnails to attract your audience.

2. **Grow Your Channel:**
   - Focus on achieving the minimum subscribers and watch-time thresholds.
   - Boost engagement with audience feedback.

3. **Check Compliance:**
   - Review the monetization policies (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).
   - Clean up any content that could violate policies.

4. **Enable 2-Step Verification:**
   - Secure your account before applying for monetization.

5. **Apply for Monetization:**
   - Head to **YouTube Studio** > **Monetization** tab.
   - Accept the Partner Program Terms.
   - Link your Google AdSense account.
   - Wait for YouTube’s team approval.

   > Approval can take up to a month but may be quicker in many cases.

6. **Monetize Content Correctly:**
   - Upon approval, customize ad preferences.
   - Ensure metadata, captions, and content format are optimized to attract ads.

---

_Tips for a successful monetization journey:_

- Focus on evergreen content that remains relevant long-term.
- Analyze channel analytics to refine your content.
- Engage with your community actively to build a loyal viewer base.
